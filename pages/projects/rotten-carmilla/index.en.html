01

Context

A band of friends, followed closely.

Rotten Carmilla began with close friends who decided to form a band. The visual identity work took place in 2025.

I followed the group's development closely: I took part in conversations about their image and the message they wanted to convey, and I was present at rehearsals.

I was commissioned and paid to create the logo, define the visual identity and develop the cover of the single “Evicted in Blood and Smoke”.

Instagram posts are among the confirmed deliverables. The story and sticker remain to be verified.

“The letters are the strokes that form the fly.”

Lettering / 2025
02

Lettering and references

The fly is the lettering.

The name appears on two lines: ROTTEN CARMILLA. The letters are heavily stylised and hard to read.

The strokes of the lettering form the fly. It is not a separate element or a drawing added afterwards: constructing the letters is constructing the fly.

Generic heavy metal logos were a reference for the difficulty of reading. Books on fly anatomy were used as a reference to draw those strokes proportionally.

Black and white, with some red details, are observed visual characteristics of the pieces. No manual, official palette or complementary typography was formally documented.
